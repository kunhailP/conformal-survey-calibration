\section{Survey applications}\label{sec:applications}

\subsection{Data and design}
We use European Social Survey rounds 9--11, which carry primary sampling unit,
stratum, region and weight variables for all 33 participating countries
\citep{essdata2024}. Curves are weighted cumulative distributions of trust in
parliament on the preregistered low-trust core $t\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, the grid of
the implementation under study; since $D$ in~\eqref{eq:D} is a maximum over
coordinates, the grid is not a cosmetic choice. Design variances come from a
Rao--Wu--Yue rescaled stratified bootstrap with $B=400$
\citep{rao1992recent,wolter2007introduction}.

\paragraph{The resampling unit.} The regional estimand is a region's departure
from its own national distribution, and region and national curves are
recomputed inside each replicate before differencing so that their covariance
propagates. The resampling unit is $(\text{stratum},\text{psu})$, never
$(\text{stratum},\text{psu},\text{region})$: a primary sampling unit spanning
two regions must enter a replicate whole, or the dependence the resample exists
to represent is destroyed.

Whether this matters is an empirical question about the design files, and we
audited all \AudCountryRounds{} country-rounds and \AudRespondents{}
respondents. Sampling units nest inside regions for all but \AudSplitShare\% of
respondents, with violations in \AudSplit{} of \AudInformative{} country-rounds
carrying informative units. Strata fail to nest in regions in
\AudStratumSplit{} of \AudCountryRounds. In \AudDegenerate{} country-rounds the
unit identifier is degenerate, equalling the respondent identifier: correct for
individual-level register samples, but it means the pooled analysis mixes
design variances that do and do not contain a clustering component, which we
report separately rather than average over.

\subsection{National estimates: the correction is unnecessary}
At the national unit the design share is \NatRhoLo--\NatRhoHi{} on full samples,
per round and for the stacked trajectory, and reaches \NatScanHi{} in a
\NatScanCells-cell subgroup scan by age band and minimum sample size. The need
gate opens in \NatGateA{} of \NatConfigs{} configurations. Whatever else is
true, there is little sampling variance to remove relative to the dispersion
between countries. Consistently with Proposition~\ref{prop:coupling}, the
dispersion term $h$ is negligible here, \NatHeteroLo--\NatHeteroHi\%: at
$K\le33$ the diagnostic is essentially its sample-size floor.

\subsection{Regional estimates: needed, and mostly unreachable}
One unit down the picture reverses. Across \RegConfigs{} configurations, two
pools by three rounds by five minimum region sizes, the design share runs
\RegRhoLo--\RegRhoHi{} and the need gate opens in \RegGateA. The reliability
gate opens in \RegGateB, and the correction activates in \RegActivations.

The sample-size floor is not the obstacle. It is cleared in
\RegFloorCleared{} of \RegConfigs{} configurations, at counts up to \RegKmax.
What binds is the coupling. The identity~\eqref{eq:identity} reproduces the
realised diagnostic to \IdErr\% mean and \IdErrMax\% maximum relative error, the
correlation between the design share and the diagnostic's inflation above its
floor is \RegCorrRhoRatio, and the closed-form boundary~\eqref{eq:boundary}
predicts the gate in \BoundCorrect{} of \RegConfigs{} configurations against
\BoundNaive{} admitted by the $K\ge94$ rule. The four activations sit where the
boundary says they can: at the smallest region size, and three of four in
round~11, the round with the lowest design share.

\subsection{Verification}
Three checks accompany the above. The conclusion does not move with the
bootstrap draw (four seeds, $D$ within $0.001$) or the replicate count
($B=200$ to $2000$, $D$ within $0.002$).

The third check separates the two departures from the inherited analysis. Under
the archive's own settings our implementation reproduces its reported diagnostic
to four decimal places, so the comparison is between the same statistic computed
two ways. The variance estimator is then decisive and the resampling unit is
not: the reliability gate opens in \VerMofmOpen{} of \VerMofmN{} $m$-of-$m$ arms
and \VerRwyOpen{} of \VerRwyN{} Rao--Wu--Yue arms, with $\tau=0.147$ falling
inside that gap, while the resampling unit moves $D$ by under
$0.1\%$ and never flips a gate. The latter follows from the design audit: with
\AudSplitShare\% of respondents in a region-splitting unit, there was little for
that correction to move. A downward-biased variance estimator makes a
correction look reachable that a defensible one does not.
